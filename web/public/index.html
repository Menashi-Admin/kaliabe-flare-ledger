Open-data flare verification · Niger Delta

Nigeria's first transparent flare tracker.

Every flare site on this map is attributed to an operator and given a verdict — verified or unverified, penalty due, methane-equivalent lost, trend — with the detection floor and uncertainty stated on each record. Built on cited open data: World Bank GFMR / NOAA VIIRS Nightfire (June 2026 release), and the Flare Gas (Prevention of Waste and Pollution) Regulations, 2018 — penalties shown at both the $2.00 and $0.50 tiers, because we do not hold production rates. Not affiliated with or endorsed by NUPRC.

Penalty basis: Flare Gas (Prevention of Waste and Pollution) Regulations, 2018 (S.I. No. 9 of 2018), Reg. 13: US$2.00 per 1,000 scf (28.317 m³) where 10,000 bbl of oil per day or more is produced, US$0.50 where less. We do not hold production rates, so every penalty is shown at both tiers (upper / lower bound); no midpoint is used. ₦ figures are indicative at one FX snapshot and are never official NUPRC assessments.

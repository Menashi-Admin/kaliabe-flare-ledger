Data sources & licences

Every figure in the ledger traces to a cited, license-clean open dataset. Nothing is scraped from proprietary services; nothing is fabricated. Live per-night VIIRS Nightfire ingest is a stub pending an EOG account.

Primary datasets

DatasetContentsLicenceAccess
NOAA VIIRS Nightfire (VNF) Nighttime flare heat signatures — location, temperature, radiance, timing. Open (NASA/NOAA); EOG Colorado School of Mines. eogdata.mines.edu / products/vnf
World Bank GFMR — Individual Flare Sites Per-site, per-year flare volumes (2012–2025), operator & field attribution. Free with attribution. worldbank.org gas flaring data page
NUPRC Flare Gas Regulations Gazette (2022) Penalty basis: US$2.00 per 1,000 scf (28.317 m³). Public government gazette. ngfcp.nuprc.gov.ng

Seed data

The current snapshot is derived from the World Bank GFMR individual-flare-site dataset (Nigeria subset), committed to this repository as data/gfmr_nigeria_sites_seed.json (476 unique sites, 2012–2025). The digest is also available from the API at /api/digest.csv.

Live ingestion (stub)

Raw per-night VIIRS Nightfire (VNF) files at eogdata.mines.edu require an Earth Observation Group account login. Until that credential exists, the ledger is fed by the published GFMR per-site dataset (which is itself VIIRS Nightfire–derived and license-clean). This is noted honestly rather than faked: nightly ingest is the documented next slice.

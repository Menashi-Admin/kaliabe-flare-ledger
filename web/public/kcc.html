Commodity cost tracker KCC 1.0

What does it roughly cost to move it?

Pick where it starts, where it goes and what it is. You get a low, central and high range per tonne, built on an open rate model. Every figure shows its source, its vintage and whether we assumed it.

Read this first. These are negotiation-planning ranges, not quotes. A haulier's price depends on season, diesel, security, queues and who you are. The band is wide on purpose: low stacks every favourable input, high stacks every unfavourable one.

Type a place from the list (add the state after a comma if two share a name), or a point as 6.52,3.37. NIPOST postcodes are not accepted live yet: the NIPOST layer is built but no API key was available, so the place grid is resolved from place names. Why.
